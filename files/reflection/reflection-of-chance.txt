THE REFLECTION OF CHANCE
A private practice from the Fellowship of Chance

Date (optional): ___________________

1. What happened?
Describe something significant or ordinary from today, without needing to judge it.


2. What was outside my control?
What circumstances, timing, other people, or chance shaped the situation?


3. Where did I have a choice?
What did I choose, or what choice may still be available?


4. What did I learn or notice?
Include uncertainty; you do not need an answer to everything.


5. What might become possible next?
Identify one realistic, compassionate next step, if any.


No one is required to complete or share a reflection. Nothing here is a test of character.
