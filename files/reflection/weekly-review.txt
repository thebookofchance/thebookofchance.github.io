A WEEK OF POSSIBILITIES
A private weekly review from the Fellowship of Chance

Week beginning (optional): ___________________

1. What surprised me?
Something I did not expect, welcome or unwelcome.


2. What did I learn about my circumstances?
Notice what helped, limited or changed my options.


3. Which choice am I glad I made?
This can be a small choice, including rest or asking for help.


4. Where did someone else make a difference?
Acknowledge support, chance encounters or collective effort.


5. What could I carry forward?
Choose one realistic intention for the coming week.


There is no perfect streak, score or requirement to share your answers.
