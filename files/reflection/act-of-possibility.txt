THE ACT OF POSSIBILITY
A small voluntary practice from the Fellowship of Chance

Date (optional): ___________________

1. Whose possibilities might I help widen?
Consider their wishes, circumstances, and ability to say no.


2. What could I offer?
An introduction, a conversation, time, a skill, a useful resource, or a thoughtful action.


3. What would make this safe, welcome and realistic?
What consent or boundaries need to be respected?


4. What one step will I try?
Choose something within your means. No money is necessary.


5. What happened, if I wish to reflect later?
An offered action is valuable even if the outcome remains uncertain.


Participation is optional. Do not give money or assistance beyond your capacity.
