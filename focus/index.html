Voluntary support, when we're ready

Fund an outcome, not a promise.

Our longer-term aim is to keep foundational literature and ordinary Fellowship membership free, while allowing people to support agreed projects if they choose. Contribution can also mean sharing a skill, helping organise a gathering, or making an introduction with consent.

01

Publish the goal

Describe what we intend to deliver, who benefits, expected costs and decision-making responsibility.

02

Protect the contribution

Establish appropriate legal and financial arrangements, oversight and treatment of purpose-restricted funds before collecting money.

03

Report the outcome

Publish the actual result and spending, including what changed and how unused funds are handled.

No donations are being accepted through this website at present. There is no active fundraising target or verified amount raised to display. The Fellowship does not claim to be a registered charity or offer tax-deductible donations.

A possible future initiative

Chance Campaigns.

There is a poetic idea worth exploring: letting chance help decide which worthy external cause receives the attention of a future community campaign.

Concept only · No selection in progress

Responsibility before randomness.

A future campaign could shortlist independently vetted external charities working on agreed causes. After eligibility and any donor restrictions are established, a publicly documented random draw could choose among genuinely comparable options.

We would publish the selection method, eligible candidates, campaign purpose and later outcome. Urgent needs should not be forced into a game of chance. Internal member assistance, if ever developed, would use safeguards and needs-based decisions rather than a random wheel.

Nothing is open for nominations, voting or funding yet. We will announce any real campaign only after its rules and safeguards are approved.
