Transparency & privacy

Interest list privacy.

A short explanation of what the optional interest forms collect, how the information is used, and how to withdraw it.

This notice is about the future Fellowship and physical-book interest lists. You do not need to provide personal details to read or download The Book of Chance.

Who is responsible?

The Book of Chance project, represented publicly by H, is responsible for the two interest lists. For privacy questions or requests, contact the project through the contact channel that will be published before registrations open.

What we collect

Fellowship interest: your email address, optional display name, confirmation that you are at least 18, and your consent to be contacted about the proposed community. A display name may be a pseudonym at this stage; this is not official membership.

Physical-book interest: your email address, preferred cover (Light or Dark), intended quantity, optional country or region, and your consent to receive print-campaign updates. This is not a purchase, deposit, reservation or binding commitment.

Why we collect it

Only to send updates relating to the list you chose. Signing up to one list does not add you to the other, and neither list is used to register you as a Fellowship Member. We do not sell contact lists or use them for unrelated marketing.

Where information goes

If the forms are activated, submissions are sent to Formspree, an external form service, and may be processed or stored outside New Zealand. You should review Formspree’s privacy policy before opting in. Authorised project administrators can view and manage submissions. The public GitHub Pages website itself does not store form entries in its repository.

Retention, access and correction

We will retain interest records only while they are needed to administer the chosen list, review them at least annually, and delete them when no longer needed. You can request access, correction, removal or an end to messages by contacting the project address shown above. You can withdraw from one list without affecting the other. Removal requests will be actioned through the form service and any associated records under our control.

Security and limits

We intend to restrict access to authorised administrators and use the form provider's access controls. No online service can promise absolute security. Please do not include personal stories, medical details, addresses, or other sensitive information in these forms.

What is not collected

No payment details, delivery address, legal identity documents or membership declarations are requested on this site. Reading preferences may be stored locally on your own device, as described in the reader; they are not sent to H by these forms.

Notice dated 22 November 2026. This page should be updated whenever collection, providers or retention practices change. You may request access to or correction of your information using the contact address above.
