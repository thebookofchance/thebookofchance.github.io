An invitation to think.

The Book of Chance is a work of reflective philosophy, not a revelation or a claim to have settled scientific debates. Its central conviction—that chance is fundamental and the future is not entirely predetermined—is presented as a philosophical position open to criticism.

Its ethical commitments begin with dignity, meaningful action, attention to unequal circumstances and responsibility toward one another and the living world. Those commitments deserve their own arguments; they cannot be derived simply from randomness.

The work draws upon prior conversations in philosophy about causation, luck, free will, care, moral responsibility and practical opportunity. Its contribution is in bringing ideas together into a distinct synthesis and a way of living with uncertainty—not claiming they were all invented here.

The author

H is the pseudonymous authorial voice of The Book of Chance. The name signals authorship, not special authority over readers or immunity from disagreement.

About the Fellowship

The book is associated with the envisioned Fellowship of Chance, a proposed community centred on reflection, growth and support for others. The book stands on its own: reading it involves no membership, loyalty, donation or other obligation.

The Fellowship is not represented here as an active membership service. Readers may explore the philosophy independently.
