The Book of Chance

A Note on This Edition

The Book of Chance introduces Confluent Humanism through reflection, stories and practical ethics. It may be read from beginning to end or discussed one reading at a time. It is not a revelation or a scientific demonstration. The parables are fictional; the arguments are offered for scrutiny.

The author writes under the pseudonym H. The name identifies its authorial voice, not a special authority over the reader.
