## Why this revision was made

The v0.1 simulated reading review assessed the manuscript from seven modelled editorial perspectives. It identified a strong central argument and sound consent and governance boundaries, but asked for greater narrative distinctiveness, more positive and imperfect examples, a clearer practical framework, more precise philosophical context and a reduction in repeated cautions. No independent readers were recruited. These are edits made in response to an AI-modelled editorial assessment, not a record of human reader testing.

## Substantive changes

1. **Chapter 4 — What Tomorrow Does Not Owe Us.** Replaced a second treatment of decision quality versus outcome (already central to Letter 02) with a fictional, reversible two-session singing experiment. It shows uncertainty, honest limits, consent, adjustment, an unexpected contribution and the legitimacy of stopping without failure.
2. **Chapter 7 — The Small Repair.** Replaced the inaccessible-building/broken-bell example, which overlapped with *The Five Values*, with a conflicting community collection timetable. The correction is trialled and reviewed with users, revealing a further access question.
3. **Chapter 9 — Fair Rules, Not Only Favours.** Replaced a near-duplicate funded-workshop-place dilemma from *Conversations of Chance* with a library outreach decision based on misleading past attendance data. The new example explores measurement, feedback, transparent trade-offs and reviewable decisions.
4. **Chapter 2 — The Difference Between an Option and a Choice.** Introduced four voluntary questions about discovery, reachability, consent/refusal and who bears costs. Added a substantial counterargument: a community choir may reasonably decline a prestigious opportunity to protect continuity and participation. This is an editorial synthesis, not a new theory or quantified index.
5. **Chapter 8 — Learning Without a Ladder.** Removed a potentially patronising laughing-classroom moment. Added reciprocal learning between an older learner making a short family film and a younger learner interpreting and cooking family recipes; neither is positioned as inherently superior.
6. **Chapter 12 — Leaving a Key Where Others Can Find It.** Made the recipient of shared knowledge an active co-designer who improves instructions for people who cannot rely on colour differences.
7. **Chapters 5–6 and 11.** Strengthened the consequences of refusal and privacy breaches, showed a group redistributing a volunteer's workload, and included an affected access user's concrete voice in the habitat-versus-path decision.
8. **Chapters 1–3 and closing practice.** Sharpened the distinction between visibility and eligibility, clarified who gets to decide what sacrifice is reasonable, removed wording potentially dismissive of fairness duties, and returned lightly to the four questions.
9. **Front and back matter.** Condensed the introductory disclosures without concealing the Fellowship's unlaunched status. Expanded definitions; distinguished practical opportunity from metaphysical indeterminacy; added optional, externally verified references to the capability approach, Iris Marion Young, relational autonomy and care ethics.
10. **Line editing and typography.** Reduced repeated cautionary phrases, tightened the longest chapters and rebalanced the print layout. All twelve chapter titles and the opening/closing chair motif are preserved.
