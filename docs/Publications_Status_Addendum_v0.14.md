# Publications Standard — Current Status Addendum (v0.14)

**22 November 2026 · Not a replacement for the Publications Standard v1.0-RC1**

The publications standard remains the current working editorial framework. Its section 10 originally described Letter 02 and Letter 03 as planned; both are now drafted, and all three Letters have received targeted cross-publication revisions. Their current editions are 1.0-RC3, 1.0-RC2, and 1.0-RC2 respectively. The book's online manuscript and downloadable editions were updated in v0.14.

Human reader pilot testing is unavailable for this project and has **not** occurred. Founder editorial approval and real deployment checks are still separate decisions. The absence of a human pilot does not prevent continuing to develop draft literature; it does prevent claims that the literature has been tested with real readers, represents reader consensus, or is certified accessible on all devices.

The next new title remains **The Five Values**. YouTube/video work remains deferred until the planned publication programme is complete.
