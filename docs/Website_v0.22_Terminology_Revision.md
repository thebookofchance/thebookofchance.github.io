# Website v0.22 — Public Language Revision

**Prepared:** 22 November 2026 · **Change class:** Editorial terminology alignment

## Intent

H requested removal of the project’s original gaming abbreviation from public literature, in favour of precise language appropriate to readers unfamiliar with gaming. This revision does not rename the philosophy or Fellowship, and does not change its philosophical commitments.

## Publications affected

- *The Book of Chance*: replaced one glossary entry with an explanation of improving practical prospects; regenerated Light/Dark PDF and EPUB files.
- *Conversations of Chance*: rewrote passages in Conversations 2 and 8 and its terms note; regenerated PDF/EPUB.
- *A Beginner’s Guide to Confluent Humanism*: removed one unnecessary analogy paragraph; regenerated PDF/EPUB.
- *Questions About Chance*: revised the unpublished FAQ separately (v0.2), not integrated into the website.

## Website changes

Regenerated source-driven HTML readings, updated search-index content and the site content manifest. Cover designs, all other publication manuscripts and the form configuration remain unchanged.

## Version status

The existing digital download paths are kept stable. This is a textual correction to **editorial candidates**, not a declaration that the works have passed independent human or accessibility review. Earlier historical materials remain archived in their original versions; the current versions supersede their vocabulary for future publication.

## Release checks

Verify live HTML, CDN/browser caching of replaced PDF/EPUB files, e-reader compatibility, cover continuity and all previously working forms after deployment. A live deployment is not established by local checks.
