# The Reflection Journal — production record v0.9

This document supersedes the 28-page v0.8 concept. The canonical collection design and publishing plan is `docs/Journal_Series_Editorial_and_Print_Plan.md`.

The main Reflection Journal has **48 pages**, with **Light and Dark** cover editions and exactly the same interior. Two focused companion publications have **40 pages each**: **The Daily Reflection Journal** and **The Possibility Journal**. All digital editions have editable multiline PDF forms and are free to download. The original three A4 worksheets remain available.

**Sources:** `scripts/build_journal_series.py` (the new publication interior, covers, and web cover images), `scripts/build_reflection_pdfs.py` (worksheet and series orchestration), and `scripts/v09_enhancements.py` (site Library and Reflect presentation).

The dedicated print-oriented main journal proof uses ruled writing areas, not interactive fields. This remains a layout study and **cannot be marketed as a print-ready, printer-approved book**. See the editorial and print plan for required production gates.

The journal prompts derive from the three existing practices and the *Turning of Chance* in *The Book of Chance*. They preserve agency, ambiguity, privacy, nonjudgmental reflection and voluntary care. No fictional testimonials or claims of publication sales have been added.
