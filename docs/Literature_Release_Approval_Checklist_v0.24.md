## Gate A — Editorial and philosophical approval

1. Establish which of the revised Foundational Identity v1.0-RC2 and Identity & Values Charter RC2 commitments H is adopting as the **current reference identity**; adopting an identity is **not** authorisation for governance, membership, money or community programmes.
2. Confirm the canonical manuscript and edition for **each** work; review the text in full, not merely a simulated reader summary. Mark story examples fictional, distinguish metaphysical beliefs from evidence and identify any factual claims requiring sources.
3. Review cross-publication terminology, the retired gaming acronym, philosophical consistency, free-will qualifications, voluntary practice, freedom of belief and realistic limits of future services.
4. Confirm attribution to H and proportionate disclosure of AI assistance. Acknowledge influences and ensure no third-party rights are infringed.
5. Obtain explicit H approval of **each manuscript and each cover**; record decisions and any exceptions. A positive design recommendation is not automatically an approval.
6. Seek feedback from actual human readers with different beliefs, ages, backgrounds and reading abilities. Simulated reader reviews are preparatory, not substitutes.

## Gate B — Production and accessibility

1. Compare **HTML, PDF and EPUB** against the approved source; check content completeness, sequence, chapter/footnote navigation, original illustrations and alt text, cover identity, version, dates and author metadata.
2. Run PDF structural and accessibility checks (including reading order, semantic tags, contrast and selectable text); evaluate with actual assistive technology and e-readers. Existing reading proofs are **not** certified accessible because they open successfully.
3. Verify on mobile and desktop, keyboard-only and screen readers; include font scaling, dark mode and no-JavaScript fallbacks as relevant.
4. Confirm external citations, licences, imprint/legal information as appropriate, and whether print-readiness is claimed (do not imply print certification without testing).

## Gate C — Explicit release and site changes

1. Record for **each title**: approved final edition, cover sign-off, approver, date, review evidence, definitive file hashes and final public status.
2. Only for an approved title, replace editorial-candidate/proof wording **with truthful final-edition language** across the online page, Library, homepage, generated source metadata, PDF/EPUB front matter, catalogue, QA reports and download labels. Do not erase the original dated revision history or silently rewrite earlier editions.
3. Remove `noindex` and add that title to `sitemap.xml` **only after** its individual release gates are passed. `noindex` is not access control.
4. Test the release package on the actual deployed GitHub Pages site, including all downloadable files, contact and form functions, responsive design and links from the Guided Reading Journey.
5. Maintain an approved release register and change log; preserve previous editorial candidates in the private archive rather than presenting them as newly published texts.

## Not authorised by literary approval

Approving books does **not** establish the Fellowship Council, registered membership, a community platform, moderator or complaints system, financial accounts, fundraising, a hardship service, mentoring or a charitable status. These are separate operational gates in the foundational identity documents.

## Sequencing recommendation

First verify the RC2 identity and final canonical manuscripts; then approve in release-sized batches, beginning with the foundational book, the three Letters, the companions, the Beginner's Guide and FAQ. Only after confirmed release approvals should the website be switched from staged candidates to final-publication statuses, followed by the live acceptance check and video launch preparation.
