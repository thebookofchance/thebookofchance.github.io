The Fellowship of Chance · A community in development

Belong somewhere.
Make possibilities together.

A conversation can change how we see our lives. A person we meet can open a door we did not know existed. What might happen if we made more room for both?

We want to build a thoughtful community where people of different generations and experiences come together to share knowledge, encourage growth, and work on things that matter.

Through chance, we meet. Through choice, we grow. Together, we create possibility.

Why belong?

Not another list of contacts.
Real relationships.

The Fellowship is not a place to advertise yourself or collect credentials. The idea is simpler: get to know one another through conversation and shared activity, and discover what those relationships can make possible.

01

Find a different perspective.

Explore questions about uncertainty, hope and change with people whose experiences differ from yours. You can disagree, learn, and still belong.

02

Learn from someone else.

Imagine an older member sharing a lifetime of lessons, and a younger member sharing a skill they have just learned. Mentorship should be a two-way exchange.

03

Do something together.

Turn shared interests into useful work: a learning session, a local project, an introduction or an act that widens someone else’s opportunities.

Small beginnings.

These are illustrations of what the Fellowship hopes to enable, not testimonials or activities that have already taken place.

01 / A conversation

Someone listens.

At a gathering, someone shares that they are uncertain about their next step. Another person offers a perspective they had not considered. Nothing is promised. A possibility appears.

02 / A shared skill

Someone teaches.

A member offers to show others how to use a tool, repair something or develop a skill. People leave with knowledge they did not have before.

03 / A joint effort

People act.

A small group identifies a local need and works together on an achievable project. The work is practical, its limitations are clear, and its impact can be reported.

An example of an achievable first goal

One well-run pilot gathering.

Before requesting funds, we could publish a small, itemised budget for an accessible, adult-only discussion event with appropriate moderation, then report what took place and what was learned. This is a possible future project, not an active fundraising campaign.

What supporters should expect

Transparency before fundraising.

Our intended standard is to publish what funds are being requested for, how they are spent and what outcomes are achieved—while protecting donor identities and the privacy of people receiving help.

✓ Public budgets and meaningful financial reporting

✓ Independent oversight and clear spending authority

✓ No purchasing influence, higher human worth or priority assistance

✓ No expectation to give beyond one's means

We are not accepting donations yet. The Fellowship first needs an appropriate legal structure, governance, finance controls and an approved plan for the use of funds. Questions worth answering

Belonging should be straightforward.

Will I have to donate to join?

No. Ordinary registered Membership is intended to be free. Financial support is separate and voluntary. Becoming a Senior Member is a later recognition process based on sustained service and giving within one's means, with hardship accommodations.

Will donations guarantee opportunities or special treatment?

No. Giving would not purchase influence, priority help or access to another person's decisions. The Fellowship cannot guarantee good fortune, connections or outcomes.

Can I contribute without money?

Absolutely. Sharing knowledge, helping with a project, listening, making introductions and volunteering are all meaningful contributions. No one should sacrifice their basic wellbeing to demonstrate commitment.

Is the Fellowship already running events or providing assistance?

Not yet. Registered Membership, official gatherings, mentoring and hardship services are development goals. The interest list is only for updates about the future opening.

Where things stand

The foundation comes first.

Before welcoming registered Members or accepting donations, we intend to establish accountable leadership, privacy and safeguarding practices, fair procedures and financial transparency.
