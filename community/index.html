Fellowship of Chance · In development

Chance brings us together.
What we do next matters.

A community imagined around a simple belief: the people we meet, the knowledge we share and the choices we make can widen one another's possibilities.

We're developing a place for genuine relationships across experiences and generations—where reflection becomes conversation, and conversation can become meaningful support.

Why a Fellowship?

Connection before opportunity.

The Fellowship isn't designed as a directory of career profiles or a place to sell yourself. Relationships should develop through shared time, questions, projects and mutual understanding.

01

Be heard. Be curious.

Discuss what you're learning, reflect on the uncertainty we all face and hear perspectives unlike your own. Disagreement is welcome; respect is expected.

02

Learn from lived experience.

Meet people with different experiences. Informal guidance and a future structured adult mentorship programme could help members navigate possibilities together.

03

Make something possible.

Bring people together around community projects, useful introductions, shared knowledge and practical acts that widen opportunities beyond ourselves.

The five values

Hope. Acceptance.
Reflection. Growth. Community.

No one value stands above the others. Each has a role in how we live with uncertainty and how we treat the people around us.

First-year development priorities

From ideas to real support.

These are the first areas we intend to develop. None should be presented as a service already running.

↗

Mentorship & guidance

Informal conversations and, when appropriate safeguards exist, structured adult-to-adult mentoring based on mutual consent.

⌁

Collaborative projects

Local and online initiatives in which people share skills, work together and improve opportunities in their communities.

◌

Practical support

Thoughtful, confidential help for members facing hardship, introduced only when the necessary safeguarding and operational capacity are in place.

How belonging is envisioned

A place for everyone to begin.

Joining is never required to read the book or consider its ideas. Participation will be voluntary, with a free membership model once the community is ready.

01

Follower

Explore the complete foundational writings openly. No registration and no obligation to agree with every idea.

02

Member

Planned free registration for adults aged 18+, with community gatherings and resources when operational. Real names will be used in protected Member spaces, not published openly.

Planned · Not open
03

Senior Member

Future recognition for sustained, meaningful service and contributions, with reasonable hardship accommodations. Seniority will never mean greater human worth.

Where things stand

The community is being built.

The Fellowship is a developing organisation, not an active member service. Registration, gatherings, mentorship and support programmes are not open yet. We want to establish trust, privacy, accountability and safeguarding before inviting people into member spaces.

You can begin as a follower simply by reading the philosophy. Updates about the wider community will appear here as plans become operational.
