Looking for a different question?

Every question in the twelve-week cycle is available here. The featured question changes by calendar week and repeats after twelve weeks; this is a curated library, not a claim that new writing appears automatically.

Browse all twelve questions
  1. An unexpected openingWhat unexpected opportunity appeared in your life recently, however small?
  2. The help you receivedWho made something easier for you without necessarily realising it?
  3. A different beginningWhich circumstances shaped a choice you made this week?
  4. What did not happenWhat hoped-for outcome failed to arrive, and what did it reveal about your expectations?
  5. A useful disagreementWhen did someone see a situation differently from you?
  6. The invisible networkWhose work, kindness or decisions made an ordinary part of your day possible?
  7. Another person's possibilityWhere might a small gesture from you widen another person's options?
  8. What you learnedWhat changed in your understanding after an experience you did not plan?
  9. Living with uncertaintyWhich uncertainty is taking more of your attention than you would like?
  10. The choice to restWhere would acceptance or rest be more useful than forcing progress?
  11. Shared growthWhat can you teach someone, and what could you genuinely learn from them?
  12. A future you can influenceWhat small action today could improve the chances of a better tomorrow?
The next possibility

Reflection can lead to action.

If a question changes what you notice, consider one small act that might widen another person's options. The Fellowship is developing shared ways to explore these practices, but you do not need to join anything to begin.
