A practice for ordinary life

Make room to notice.

The Reflection of Chance is an invitation to look at what happened, what you could influence and what might become possible next. Not a test, a streak, or a demand for certainty.

A practice you can make your own

Three ways to begin.

Write a few sentences or take your time. You can keep every word to yourself. A reflection can be useful even if it ends with a question.

01 / Daily

The Reflection of Chance

Notice circumstances, choice, learning and the future without blaming yourself for what you could not control.

02 / Action

The Act of Possibility

Consider one voluntary way you might help another person or the living world, with consent and within your means.

03 / Weekly

A Week of Possibilities

Look across the week for surprises, learning, other people's contributions and something worth carrying forward.

The next possibility

Reflection can lead to action.

If a question changes what you notice, consider one small act that might widen another person's options. The Fellowship is developing shared ways to explore these practices, but you do not need to join anything to begin.
